How to use

Drag cards to arrange the board.

Choose Link cards, then click two cards. Repeat the same pair to remove a link.

Keyboard: Tab to a card, arrow keys to move it, Enter to select it in link mode.

Saves stay in this browser. Export downloads a backup.
